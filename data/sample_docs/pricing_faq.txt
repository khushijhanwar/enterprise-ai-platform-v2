Pricing FAQ - Acme Cloud Services

Acme Cloud Services offers three tiers: Starter, Growth, and Enterprise.
The Enterprise tier includes volume discounts starting at 100,000 API
calls per month, dedicated support with a one-hour SLA for critical
incidents, and custom contract terms. Growth tier customers are billed
monthly based on usage, with no long-term commitment required. Starter
tier is free for the first 10,000 API calls per month, after which
standard pay-as-you-go pricing applies. All tiers include access to the
platform's core AI agent capabilities, though advanced workflow
orchestration features are Enterprise-only. Enterprise contracts are
billed annually with net-30 payment terms.

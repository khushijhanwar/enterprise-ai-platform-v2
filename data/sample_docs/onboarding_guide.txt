Enterprise Onboarding Guide - Acme Cloud Services

New enterprise accounts go through a four-stage onboarding process. Stage
one is a kickoff call with a solutions architect to review technical
requirements and integration points. Stage two is environment
provisioning, including VPC setup, IAM roles, and data pipeline
configuration. Stage three is data migration, which typically completes
within two to three weeks depending on data volume and source system
complexity. Stage four is the go-live review and handoff to the customer
success team. Each enterprise account is assigned a dedicated Customer
Success Manager who acts as the primary point of contact for escalations,
quarterly business reviews, and renewal discussions. Average time to
full onboarding completion is six weeks for standard deployments and up
to twelve weeks for accounts requiring custom integrations.

Refund Policy - Acme Cloud Services

Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of purchase if they are
unsatisfied with the service. Refunds are not available for usage-based
charges already incurred, such as compute hours or storage consumed. To
request a refund, customers must submit a ticket via the support portal
with their account ID and invoice number. Refund requests are typically
processed within 5-7 business days. Enterprise customers with an annual
contract should instead refer to the terms in their Master Services
Agreement, as the standard refund policy does not apply to annual
commitments. Partial refunds may be issued at the discretion of the
account manager in cases of documented service outages exceeding the
contractual SLA.
